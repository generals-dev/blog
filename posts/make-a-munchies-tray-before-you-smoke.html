Make a munchies tray before you smoke.

If you want snacks that actually satisfy, plan a small station: something crunchy, something creamy, something sweet, plus a hydrating drink. Portion everything into small bowls or containers so you don’t snack straight from a package. Include one fresh item to balance heavy flavors and add texture.

The lesson: contrast and portioning stop overeating. For specific snack combos and tried ideas, check https://kushstation.co/weed-munchies/
