Cannabis Overdose Symptoms: When to Get Help Fast

November 14, 2026

Too much THC can cause a racing heart, nausea, vomiting, dizziness, confusion, paranoia and poor coordination. This guide covers the warning signs of cannabis overdose, how long effects last for smoking compared with edibles, what to do while someone recovers, and when to call 911 or a poison centre. It also covers risks for children, why naloxone won’t reverse THC, and conditions that can look like greening out, plus tips to help prevent it.
